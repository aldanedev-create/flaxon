Northstar Academy is a community of teachers, students, and families who believe that education should make people more capable and more generous.

Our mission

Make room for every student's best work.

We combine strong academics with the habits that help young people thrive: asking thoughtful questions, working with others, taking responsibility, and returning to the work when it gets difficult.

Our school is deliberately personal. Students are known by their teachers, supported by their advisors, and invited to contribute to the life of the community.
